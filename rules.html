Responsible Cybersecurity

📋 Cybersecurity Rules

01 — Authorization

Only test systems when you have permission.

02 — Safe Labs

Practice in your own lab or authorized training environment.

03 — Privacy

Respect personal information and confidential data.

04 — No Damage

Do not intentionally damage or disrupt systems.

05 — Protect Credentials

Never steal or misuse passwords or accounts.

06 — No Malware

Do not create or distribute harmful software.

07 — Responsible Testing

Keep security experiments inside authorized environments.

08 — Defensive Learning

Focus on protecting systems and understanding risks.

09 — Responsible Reporting

Use the owner's approved process when reporting vulnerabilities.

10 — Keep Learning

Build your skills responsibly and ethically.
